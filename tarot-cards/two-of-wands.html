Two of Wands tarot card meaning

Minor Arcana · Wands · Fire

conflictdecisionoptionindividuality

Two of Wands is one of the 14 cards of the suit of Wands, the suit of Fire in the Minor Arcana, which speaks of energy, drive, ambition and creative work. Its keywords are conflict, decision, option and individuality.

Two of Wands upright meaning

In the light, Two of Wands points to:

  • Having a choice
  • Offering or being offered an option
  • Seeing the value of another person's approach
  • Understanding there's more than one way to "skin a cat"
  • Successfully doing more than one thing at a time
  • Being empowered to make a choice

Two of Wands reversed or shadow meaning

In its shadow, drawn reversed or in a difficult position, Two of Wands can point to:

  • Misrepresenting your intentions
  • Doing one thing while desiring another
  • Changing course mid-stream for no good reason
  • Refusing to change your goal even when pursuing it no longer makes sense
  • Disregarding the input of others

Two of Wands in fortune telling

  • Beware false friends
  • Don't be mealy-mouthed; say what you think and do what you want to do
